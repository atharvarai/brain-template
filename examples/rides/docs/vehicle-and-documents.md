# Vehicle and Documents

## Documents you need

- Driving licence
- Registration certificate (RC)
- Insurance
- Pollution under control (PUC) certificate

Upload renewed documents at least 15 days before the old ones expire. If a document expires, your account is paused until the new one is approved.

## Vehicle age

| Service | Maximum vehicle age |
| --- | --- |
| Zipvaha Go | 8 years |
| Zipvaha Prime | 5 years |

Vehicle age is counted from the registration date on the RC.
