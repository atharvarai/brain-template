# Ratings and Cancellations

## Your rating

Your rating is the average of the ratings riders gave on your last 100 rides.

## Low ratings

If your rating falls below 4.2, your account is suspended for 7 days and you must complete the online service training before you can drive again. You can appeal the suspension (see Deactivation and Appeals).

## Rider cancellations

If a rider cancels after you have waited 5 minutes at the pickup point, the rider pays a cancellation fee of Rs 40. You receive Rs 30 of it.

## Driver cancellations

Cancelling accepted rides hurts riders. If more than 10% of the rides you accept in a week are cancelled by you, you are not eligible for that week's ride bonus.
