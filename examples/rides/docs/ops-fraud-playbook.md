# Ops Fraud Playbook

Internal to the operations team. Do not share with drivers.

## GPS spoofing signals

A driver is flagged automatically when 3 or more trips in 7 days deviate from the expected route by more than 40%. Flagged accounts go to manual review within 24 hours.

## Manual review

Check the trip traces, the rider complaints and the device history. Do not tell the driver which signal flagged them.

## Goodwill payouts

Ops agents may approve a goodwill payout of up to Rs 500 without a manager's approval, for example when a payout error was Zipvaha's fault.
