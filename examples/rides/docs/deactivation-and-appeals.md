# Deactivation and Appeals

## Suspension and deactivation

- Temporary suspension: for a rating below 4.2 (7 days), or for expired documents (until they are renewed).
- Permanent deactivation: for safety incidents, fraud such as GPS manipulation or fake trips, or sharing your account with another person.

## How to appeal

1. Open Help in the driver app and choose Appeal a decision.
2. Explain what happened and attach any evidence, such as trip screenshots.

You must appeal within 14 days of the decision. You get one appeal per decision.

## What happens next

The appeals team sends a decision within 5 business days. If the appeal succeeds, your account is restored and any suspension is removed from your record.
