# Fares, Tolls and Fees

## Tolls and parking

Tolls and parking paid during a ride are reimbursed in full. Add them to the trip in the driver app within 24 hours of the ride ending, with a photo of the receipt. Claims added later are not reimbursed.

## Airport pickups

An airport pickup fee of Rs 50 is charged to the rider and passed to you in full.

## Night rides

Rides that start between 11 pm and 5 am carry a 20% night surcharge, which is part of the fare and commissioned like the rest of it.
