# Incentives and Bonuses

Bonuses Zipvaha pays on top of fares.

## Weekly ride bonus

Complete enough rides from Monday to Sunday to earn a weekly bonus:

| Rides in the week | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 40 to 59 | Rs 1,200 |
| 60 or more | Rs 2,200 |

The bonuses do not add up: 60 rides earns Rs 2,200, not Rs 3,400.

## Eligibility

To get the weekly ride bonus you need both:

- An acceptance rate of at least 80% for the week
- A driver cancellation rate of 10% or less for the week

Rides you cancel yourself do not count towards the target.

## Peak hour bonus

You earn Rs 25 extra for every ride that starts between 8 and 10 am or between 6 and 9 pm, Monday to Friday. Peak hour bonuses have no acceptance or cancellation conditions.

## When bonuses are paid

All bonuses for a week are paid with that week's payout on Tuesday.
