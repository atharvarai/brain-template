# Payouts

How and when Zipvaha pays drivers. Zipvaha is a fictional company; all figures are synthetic.

## Weekly payout

Earnings for rides from Monday to Sunday are paid the following Tuesday. The money reaches your bank account within 2 business days of the payout date.

## What you earn per ride

You keep the fare minus Zipvaha's commission of 18%. Tolls, parking and the airport pickup fee are added on top and are not commissioned.

## Minimum payout

The minimum weekly payout is Rs 500. If your balance is below Rs 500 on payout day, it rolls over to the next week. Nothing is lost.

## Instant cashout

You can cash out your balance before Tuesday with instant cashout:

- The fee is Rs 15 per cashout.
- You can cash out at most 2 times a day.
- The minimum instant cashout is Rs 200.

## Payout statement

Each payout comes with a statement listing rides, gross fares, commission, incentives and reimbursements. Find it in the Earnings tab of the driver app.
