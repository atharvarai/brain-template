# Support and Payout Disputes

## Driver helpline

The driver helpline is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, from the Help tab in the app.

## Disputing a payout

If a payout looks wrong (a missing bonus, a missing toll, a wrong commission), raise a dispute within 30 days of that payout. Say which week it concerns, what is wrong and the amount you expected.

Disputes are resolved within 7 business days. A correction is paid with the next weekly payout.
